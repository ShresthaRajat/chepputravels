UNESCO World Heritage

The seven monument zones of the Kathmandu Valley

The valley was inscribed on the World Heritage List in 1979 as a single site made up of seven monument zones. Much was damaged in the 2015 Gorkha earthquake, and restoration has been a long, community-led effort that you can still see in progress.

  • Kathmandu

    Kathmandu Durbar Square

    The old royal palace complex of Hanuman Dhoka, surrounded by temples and the Kumari Ghar, where the living goddess Kumari resides. Kasthamandap, the wooden rest house said to have given Kathmandu its name, collapsed in 2015 and has since been rebuilt using traditional materials and methods.

    Best time: early morning, when locals make offerings before the square fills up.

  • Lalitpur

    Patan Durbar Square

    The most compact and arguably the finest of the three squares, with the stone Krishna Mandir, the Golden Window and the palace courtyards. Keshav Narayan Chowk houses the Patan Museum, one of the best collections of Nepali sacred art.

    Best time: late afternoon, ending on the temple steps as the light turns gold.

  • Bhaktapur

    Bhaktapur Durbar Square

    Home to the 55-Window Palace and its gilded Golden Gate. Nearby Taumadhi Square holds the five-tiered Nyatapola temple, built in 1702 and still standing after the great earthquakes of 1934 and 2015.

    Best time: stay into the evening, after day-trippers leave the squares.

  • Boudha

    Boudhanath

    A vast white dome crowned by the watchful eyes of the Buddha and ringed by monasteries, many founded by Tibetan communities. Its spire was damaged in 2015 and rebuilt within two years, largely through donations from devotees.

    Best time: dusk, when pilgrims walk the kora and butter lamps are lit.

  • West Kathmandu

    Swayambhunath

    A hilltop stupa sacred to both Buddhists and Hindus, with a history of worship going back more than 1,500 years. Visitors know it as the Monkey Temple. A long stone stairway climbs to the summit and a wide view over the city.

    Best time: sunrise, with morning prayers and the valley below in mist.

  • Bagmati River

    Pashupatinath

    The holiest Shiva temple in Nepal, set on the banks of the Bagmati. Only Hindus may enter the main temple, but visitors can watch from the opposite bank, where open-air cremations take place at the ghats. Please be respectful here.

    Best time: the evening aarti, from around 6pm depending on the season.

  • Bhaktapur District

    Changu Narayan

    Widely considered the oldest temple in the valley, on a ridge above Bhaktapur. A stone pillar inscription here dates to 464 CE, and the two-tiered temple is a gallery of Licchavi-era sculpture and carved struts.

    Best time: morning, then walk down to Bhaktapur for lunch.

Beyond the Valley

Half-day to two-day escapes

Hills, villages and monasteries within easy reach of the city. Distances and drive times are approximate from Thamel and depend heavily on traffic.

  • Nagarkot

    A ridge on the valley rim known for sunrise over the Himalaya. On clear mornings, mostly October to March, the view stretches across a long arc of snow peaks.

    ~32 km · 1.5 to 2 hrs · overnight recommended

  • Changu Narayan

    The valley's oldest temple, on a quiet hilltop. Pairs well with a walk or drive on to Nagarkot or Bhaktapur.

    ~22 km · about 1 hr

  • Kirtipur

    A hilltop Newar town of brick lanes and temples, with the Bagh Bhairab temple and views back across Kathmandu.

    ~8 km · 30 to 45 mins

  • Bungamati & Khokana

    Twin Newari villages south of Patan. Bungamati is known for woodcarving, Khokana for its traditional mustard oil pressing.

    ~12 km · about 45 mins

  • Namobuddha

    A hilltop pilgrimage site and Tibetan Buddhist monastery, tied to a legend of the Buddha offering his body to a starving tigress.

    ~40 km · 2 to 2.5 hrs

  • Dakshinkali

    A forested temple to the goddess Kali at the southern edge of the valley, busiest on Tuesday and Saturday mornings.

    ~22 km · 1 to 1.5 hrs

  • Shivapuri Nagarjun National Park

    Forest trails on the valley's northern rim. A full-day hike can reach Shivapuri peak or the Nagi Gompa nunnery.

    ~12 km to the Budhanilkantha gate · about 45 mins

  • Chandragiri Hills

    A cable car climbs from Thankot to a hilltop temple with wide views of the valley and, on clear days, the Himalaya.

    ~15 km · 45 mins to 1 hr

  • Pharping

    A small town of caves and monasteries important to Tibetan Buddhists, often combined with Dakshinkali.

    ~19 km · about 1 hr

A Day in Kathmandu with Cheppu

From sunrise prayers to a Newari feast

A sample day showing how we pace things: early starts at the quiet hours, a proper lunch, and the sacred sites when they come alive in the evening.

  1. 06:00

    Sunrise at Swayambhunath

    Climb to the stupa as pilgrims spin prayer wheels and the city wakes below.

  2. 08:30

    Back down the hill for sel roti, tea and a briefing on the day ahead.

  3. 09:30

  4. 11:30

  5. 13:00

    Lunch in Patan

    Patan Museum and artisans

    Sacred art in the old palace, then a metal-casting workshop nearby.

  7. 17:00

    Boudhanath at dusk

    Join the clockwise kora as butter lamps are lit around the stupa.

    Aarti at Pashupatinath

    Fire, bells and chanting on the banks of the Bagmati.

  9. 20:00

    Newari feast

    Samay baji, chatamari and seasonal yomari to close the day.

Festivals Calendar

Time your trip to a jatra

Festival dates follow the lunar calendar and change every year. Tell us your travel window and we will check the dates for you.

  • April

    Bisket Jatra

    Bhaktapur's New Year festival, with chariots hauled through the streets and a towering pole raised and felled.

    Bhaktapur

  • Apr to Jun

    Rato Machhindranath Jatra

    A towering chariot pulled through Patan over several weeks, one of the valley's longest festivals.

    Patan (Lalitpur)

  • May

    Buddha Jayanti

    The Buddha's birth, enlightenment and passing, marked with processions, prayer flags and lamps.

    Boudhanath & Swayambhunath

  • September

    Indra Jatra

    Masked dances, the raising of the Indra pole and the chariot procession of the living goddess Kumari.

    Kathmandu Durbar Square

  • Oct to Nov

    Dashain & Tihar

    Nepal's biggest festivals: family blessings and tika at Dashain, then lamps, garlands and the festival of lights at Tihar.

    Across the valley
