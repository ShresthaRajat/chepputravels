Every trip is different. Here are a few useful starting points.

Can you create a custom itinerary? +

Yes. Use the enquiry form to share your interests, dates and preferred pace. The team can then discuss a suitable itinerary with you.

Are the prices shown online? +

Package prices have not been configured yet. We use “Custom quote” until actual pricing, inclusions and terms are confirmed.

Can I book and pay online? +

This first version is enquiry-based. Bookings and payments are not processed on the website, and no booking is confirmed by submitting the form.

How do I know a trip is right for me? +

Tell us about your interests, fitness or mobility needs, travel dates and budget in your enquiry so those details can be discussed before confirming a trip.
